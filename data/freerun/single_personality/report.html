Divergence from human negotiators (kodis_stats.py)

Procedure after the KODIS evaluation scripts (strategic_behavior.py / run_evaluation.py), with two corrections to the tests. ‡ distribution-level metrics: within-human vs agent-vs-human pairwise distances; tested by permuting dyads (4,999 permutations; within-GameId swaps for replay rows), not by a t-test over the pairwise values, which treats ~1,000-2,000 dependent pairs as independent and gave 43-50% false positives on these data. † dyad-level metrics: paired t-test on GameId for replay rows (each replay dyad answers the same real dyad), Welch t-test for free-run rows. Gaps are signed and shown relative to the human baseline. Human baseline for every row and every table: 47 real dyads with an identifiable BATNA recipient (data/human_data.csv). All rows are coded by the same GPT-5.4 classifier.
A non-significant gap is not evidence of alignment: it means no gap was detectable at this sample size. An equivalence claim needs an equivalence test (TOST; Lakens 2017). A ↓ cell (agent closer to the humans than humans are to each other) indicates under-dispersion, not better alignment -- check the dispersion table.
